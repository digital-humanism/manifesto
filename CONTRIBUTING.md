# Contributing to the Digital Humanism Manifesto

Thank you for your interest in this manifesto. This document belongs to humanity,
not to any single author or corporation.

## How You Can Contribute

### Translations
We welcome translations into any language. If you translate this manifesto:
1. Create a new file: `README.[lang-code].md` (e.g., `README.fr.md` for French)
2. Add a link to your translation in the language switcher at the top of every `README` file
3. Maintain the philosophical depth and manifesto tone of the original
4. Include the Glossary of Key Concepts with culturally relevant bridges where appropriate

### Improvements
If you believe a principle can be refined or expanded:
1. Open an Issue describing your proposed change
2. Explain how it aligns with the manifesto's core philosophy
3. Wait for community discussion before submitting a Pull Request

### Cultural Bridges
The Glossary includes "Cultural Bridges" that connect manifesto concepts to
local philosophical traditions. We especially welcome contributions that add
cultural bridges for:
- African philosophical traditions
- Indigenous knowledge systems
- South Asian epistemologies
- Latin American thought traditions

## Principles of Contribution
- Maintain the spirit of human subjectivity and cognitive sovereignty
- Respect cultural diversity and avoid imposing any single moral framework
- Preserve the manifesto's declarative, elevated tone
- Do not introduce corporate or commercial interests into the text
